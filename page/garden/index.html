We welcome prearranged visits to our garden, either groups, individuals, or a few people. Butterflies need warm, preferably sunny weather with not too much wind. That makes it almost impossible to book ahead with confidence! We can however always rearrange the date if the weather is not suitable. Afternoon is best in general and there is very little point in coming in the evenings, even in high summer. Butterflies are on the wing from April to October, with the majority flying during June, July, and August, but it depends entirely on what you want to see. Arrange your visit via our contact email: contact@homeforbutterflies.com
